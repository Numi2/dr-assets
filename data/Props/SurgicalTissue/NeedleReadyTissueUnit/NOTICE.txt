DrAnmar Needle-Ready Tissue Unit 2.2.0

The base geometry, topology, semantic sets, material seeds, and OpenUSD
authoring code covered by `asset_manifest.json` were independently authored by
the Dr.Anmar project. No third-party patient mesh, tissue mesh, or patient
texture is included.

The separately scoped render-only package covered by `visual_manifest.json`
includes byte-identical NVIDIA OpenPBR 1.1 MaterialX and medium-skin normal
inputs from PhysicalAI-SimReady-Materials v0.2.0 under MIT-0. Its nested
license, provenance, archive hash, and member hashes are retained under
`visual/vendor/`. The NVIDIA normal contributes bounded visual microstructure
only and has no physics, collision, task, or clinical authority.

The runtime architecture is designed for NVIDIA Isaac Lab (BSD-3-Clause),
NVIDIA Newton (Apache-2.0), and NVIDIA Warp (Apache-2.0). NVIDIA
SoftMimicGen (Apache-2.0) was reviewed for surgical deformable-task
organization; no SoftMimicGen code or geometry was copied. CRESSim-MPM
(BSD-3-Clause) is named only as a future topology-capable research backend
candidate; no CRESSim-MPM code is included here.

This is a research simulation asset. It is not clinically validated and is not
for patient care or clinical decision-making.
