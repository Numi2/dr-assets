# Rejected needle-thread experiments (retired)

This folder retains two rejected task-specific strand experiments outside the
selectable Needle catalog:

- `dranmar_needle_thread_fem.usda`: the surface-FEM ribbon strand;
- `dranmar_needle_v030_thread_adapter_rejected.usda`: the attempted adapter
  between the qualified puncture needle and the violet 120-segment Bench Setup
  v0.3 extended strand.

The asset completed a numerical pullout receipt but its ribbon-like collision
surface caused severe transient tissue deformation in real RTX renders. Do
not use it for puncture, pullout, training, or README demonstrations.

The v0.3 adapter was also rejected after its violet strand and reset-time
contact impulse visibly deformed and displaced the otherwise unchanged tissue.
The canonical v0.3 Bench Setup assets remain in `NeedleThread/`; only this
task-specific adapter is archived here.
